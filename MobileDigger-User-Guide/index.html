EVERY FEATURE

Powerful where it counts

A deep look at what MobileDigger does — and how each piece helps you dig faster.

CORE

Swipe-to-sort triage

The fastest way to clear a crate. The player card follows your finger and tints green or red to preview the action.

  • Swipe right → Like — moves the file to Liked and advances
  • Swipe left → Reject — moves it to Rejected and advances
  • Buttons too: 👎 Dislike, ↩ Undo, ❤ Like
  • Works on the main player and the floating mini-player
ANALYSIS

Spectrogram & waveform

Don't trust the file extension — trust your eyes. The spectrogram maps frequency, time and loudness so a transcoded MP3's cutoff is obvious.

  • Frequency (kHz), time, and dB axes
  • First 2 minutes analyzed, colour = intensity
  • Tap-and-drag waveform to seek anywhere
  • Export the whole analysis as a shareable image
ORGANIZE

Tabs, subfolders & multi-select

Three tabs — To Do, Liked, Rejected — keep your piles clean. Group likes into named subfolders and act on many tracks at once.

  • Subfolder headers show "Name · N Tracks" with select-all
  • Multi-select Like / Reject / Move / Rename
  • Rows fade & collapse instantly as files move
  • Move or batch-move into any subfolder
FIND

Search, sort & quality filters

Find any track across all three tabs, sort six ways, or filter to just the formats you care about.

  • Search by name across To Do / Liked / Rejected
  • Sort by name, size, date, duration, type, subfolder or shuffle
  • Lossless only or Lossy (MP3/AAC) only filters
  • A red dot marks when a filter is active
EVERYWHERE

Background & notification player

Lock your phone and keep digging. Full media controls live in the notification, lock screen, and on your Bluetooth headset.

  • Play/pause, next, previous & a seek bar
  • Like & Reject right from the notification
  • AirPods: double-tap to skip, single to pause
  • Album art & live progress
PERSONALIZE

33 themes, fonts & visuals

Make MobileDigger yours with a deep settings panel — every theme has tuned light and dark variants.

  • 33 colour themes + 11 bundled fonts
  • Adjustable waveform height & colour
  • Animation style & speed control
  • Behaviour toggles: auto-play, auto-scan, labels
SHARE

Share tracks, lists & analysis

Send a gem to a friend or export your finds — sharing targets WhatsApp first, with a normal share sheet fallback.

  • Share a single track (zipped, so WhatsApp accepts it)
  • Share your whole Liked folder as one ZIP
  • Export the Liked list as a clean TXT
  • Share the spectrogram analysis as an image

HOW-TO GUIDE

Everything, step by step

1 Getting started

MobileDigger sorts a folder of music into Liked and Rejected piles. First, tell it where your music is and where sorted files should go.

  1. Tap Source and pick the folder of unsorted music (it scans that folder and its subfolders).
  2. Tap Destination and pick where sorted files should land — the app creates Liked and Rejected subfolders there.
  3. Your tracks load into the To Do tab, ready to triage.
  4. Use Rescan to reload the last source, or pick from your Recent Sources list.
⚠️
Heads up: your Destination must not be inside your Source folder (sorted files would get re-scanned). The app warns you if so. You also need a Destination set before you can Like/Reject.

2 Playing & the player

Tap any track in the list to play it. The big player card at the top shows the current track and its controls.

  • ⏮ / ▶⏸ / ⏭ — previous, play/pause, next.
  • Waveform — tap or drag it to seek to any point. Toggle it with Show/Hide Wave.
  • Top line shows duration · bitrate · file size.
  • A mini-player appears at the bottom when the playing track scrolls out of view, so you always keep controls.
💡
Formats: MP3, WAV, FLAC, AIFF, AAC, OGG, M4A and more all play — the app picks the right audio engine automatically.

🎵 The mini-player

A floating player that keeps your controls handy when the big player isn't in view.

  • When it appears: automatically when the playing track scrolls off-screen, or whenever you hide the main player.
  • Controls: previous / play-pause / next, plus Dislike 👎 and Like ❤, a SpeK spectrogram toggle, and a compact, seekable waveform.
  • Swipe it — left to reject, right to like — exactly like the big player.
  • Close it with the ✕; it stays hidden until the track changes.
⚙️
Settings: toggle it with Enable Mini Player, and set its waveform size with the Mini Height slider — both under Settings → Visual.

3 Liking & rejecting

This is the heart of the app. Two ways to sort the current track:

  1. Swipe the player — right to Like (card tints green), left to Reject (tints red). Both move the file and jump to the next track.
  2. Tap the buttons — 👎 Dislike, ↩ Undo, ❤ Like.
💡
Button vs swipe: the Dislike button auto-advances, but the Like button keeps you on the track (press Next yourself). Swiping in either direction always advances. Made a mistake? Tap Undo.

↩ Reject Listened & Undo

  • Reject Listened — the orange "Reject N" pill in the header bins every track you've played but never liked or rejected. Great after auditioning a stack and skipping the duds: one tap clears them all to Rejected.
  • Undo — the ↩ button on the player reverses your last sort and restores the file to where it came from.

✎ Renaming files

  • The current track: tap the Rename (pencil) icon in the toolbar.
  • Any track: long-press its row → "Rename This File".
  • A whole multi-selection: in multi-select, tap Rename (applies a case style to all).

Every rename offers case styles: All Words With Capital Letter, ALL LETTERS UP, or all letters small. The file extension is always preserved.

4 Tabs & the workflow

Three tabs organise your session:

  • To Do — your unsorted queue.
  • Liked — tracks you kept (in the Liked folder + any subfolders).
  • Rejected — tracks you discarded.

Liking or rejecting physically moves the file and shifts it between tabs. You can re-sort from inside any tab, and Undo restores the most recent move.

5 Subfolders & moving

Keep your likes tidy by filing them into named subfolders (e.g. Techno, Disco).

  1. On the playing track, tap the folder button to open Move to Subfolder.
  2. Pick an existing subfolder, or Create new subfolder.
  3. In the Liked tab, subfolders appear as headers — "Name · N Tracks" — and you can filter the view to specific subfolders.

6 Multi-select (batch actions)

Act on many tracks at once.

  1. Enter multi-select via the Select icon, a long-press on a row, or a subfolder header checkbox.
  2. Tap rows to check them, or use Select All.
  3. Use the action bar: Reject, Like, Move, or Rename.
💡
When you Reject or Like a selection, the rows fade out and collapse immediately while the files move in the background — no blocking spinner. Any file that can't move is put back.

7 Search, sort & filters

  • Search — tap the search icon, type, then press Enter or the magnifier. It searches names across all three tabs. Tap a result to jump to it.
  • Sort — open the sort menu: Name, File Size, Date Added, Duration, Extension, Subfolder, or Shuffle. Tap again to flip direction.
  • Quality filters — Lossless Only or Lossy Only (MP3/AAC…). A red dot on the sort icon means a filter/sort is active.
  • Clear Sort & Filter resets everything.

8 Spectrogram analysis

Check a track's real quality before you keep it.

  1. Tap the spectrogram button on the player (or "Generate Spectrogram").
  2. Read the image: up = frequency (kHz), across = time, colour = loudness. A hard horizontal cutoff = a transcoded/lossy file.
  3. It analyses the first 2 minutes. The waveform sits right below.
  4. Tap the share icon to export the whole analysis as an image.

9 Notification & headset

You don't need the app open to keep working.

  • Notification / lock screen: previous, Like, play/pause, Reject, next, plus a seek bar.
  • Bluetooth / AirPods: single-press = play/pause, double-tap = next, triple = previous.
  • Playback continues in the background; swiping the notification away asks before stopping.

10 Sharing

  • One track — the Share button zips the file (so WhatsApp accepts it) and sends it.
  • Whole Liked folder — export every liked track as a single dated ZIP (with progress + cancel).
  • Liked list — export a clean numbered TXT of your finds.
  • Spectrogram — share the analysis image.

11 Cleaning up

When you've sorted everything, free up space.

  • Use Delete Rejected to permanently remove everything in the Rejected folder (with a live progress bar).
  • When the To Do list empties, the app offers to delete the Rejected pile for you.
⚠️
Permanent: deleting rejected files removes them from storage — there's no trash/undo for this.

12 Themes & settings

Open Settings to personalise everything.

  • Theme — choose from 33 themes (each with light & dark variants).
  • Dark Mode toggle; Font picker (11 bundled fonts).
  • Visual tab — waveform heights & colour, animation style & speed, mini-player toggle.
  • Behaviour — auto-play first track, auto-scan last source on start, toolbar labels.
⚠️
Themes look like they do nothing? Turn "Enable Theme" ON (i.e. turn Dynamic Color OFF). On Android 12+, dynamic colour pulls from your wallpaper and overrides the chosen theme.

⚡ Gesture cheat-sheet

  • Swipe player right → Like & advance
  • Swipe player left → Reject & advance
  • Tap & drag waveform → seek
  • Long-press a row → rename or enter multi-select
  • Tap a row → play that track
  • Double-tap AirPods → next track
